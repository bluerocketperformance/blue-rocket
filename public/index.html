About Us

Specialist work for high-performance machines.

Blue Rocket Performance is a Portland, Oregon shop focused on high-performance motorcycles and cars.

We handle everything from routine service and diagnostics to engine work, suspension setup, ECU tuning, and one-off custom fabrication. We specialize in Ducati, BMW, and other performance sportbikes, along with custom Subaru STI parts and builds.

If it needs to be repaired, tuned, or built from scratch, we can help — done right, and done to spec.
